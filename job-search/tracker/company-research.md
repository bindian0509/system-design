## Questions to Ask Companies

### About the Role
1. What does success look like in the first 90 days?
2. What's the biggest challenge the team is facing right now?
3. How is performance measured for this role?
4. What's the team structure and how would I fit in?

### About Engineering Culture
1. How do you balance technical debt vs feature development?
2. What does the on-call look like?
3. How are architectural decisions made?
4. What's the deployment frequency?

### About Growth
1. What's the path for growth in this role?
2. How do you support engineering managers' development?
3. What happened to the last person in this role?

### About Company
1. What's the company's runway/financial health?
2. What are the biggest priorities for next year?
3. How has the team/company changed in the last year?

### Red Flag Questions (ask carefully)
1. How many people have left the team in the last year?
2. What's the typical work-life balance like?
3. How does the company handle underperformers?
