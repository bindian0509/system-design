# Job Search Toolkit 🎯

A comprehensive 3-month job search preparation system for Senior Engineering Manager / Staff+ IC roles.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Fill in your details** in the resume and STAR stories templates
2. **Start applying** using the company tracker
3. **Follow the weekly rhythm** outlined below
4. **Review company-specific checklists** before each interview

## 3-Month Timeline

### Month 1: Foundation + Warm-up Interviews

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1-2 | Resume & Profile | Fill in resume template, optimize LinkedIn, calculate metrics |
| 2 | Applications | Apply to Tier 4 companies (practice interviews) |
| 3-4 | Prep Intensify | DSA daily, 1 system design per week, fill STAR stories |
| 4 | Interviews Begin | Tier 3 (Foreign MNCs) + Tier 4 interviews |

### Month 2: Peak Interview Phase

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|------|-------|---------|
| 5-6 | Big Tech Applications | Apply to Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Uber |
| 6-7 | Tier 1 Applications | Apply to PhonePe, CRED, Swiggy, Flipkart |
| 7-8 | Active Interviewing | Multiple interview loops in progress |

### Month 3: Close Offers

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|------|-------|---------|
| 9-10 | Final Rounds | Complete onsite/virtual loops |
| 11 | Offers & Negotiation | Use negotiation guide to maximize comp |
| 12 | Decision | Accept best offer, notify other companies |

## Priority Actions This Week

### If You're Just Starting
1. [ ] Fill in `resume/achievements-quantified.md` with your Razorpay numbers
2. [ ] Complete `resume/resume-master.md` with your details
3. [ ] Update LinkedIn using `resume/linkedin-profile-optimization.md`
4. [ ] Fill in first 5 STAR stories in `behavioral/star-stories-bank.md`
5. [ ] Apply to 3-5 Tier 4 companies from `tracker/company-tracker.md`

### If You Have Interviews Scheduled
1. [ ] Review company-specific checklist in `interview-prep/company-checklists.md`
2. [ ] Refresh relevant system design from `system-design/quick-reference-sheets.md`
3. [ ] Review 5-6 STAR stories most relevant to company values
4. [ ] Do 2-3 warm-up coding problems from `coding/coding-patterns-guide.md`
5. [ ] Prepare questions to ask interviewers

### If You Have Offers
1. [ ] Use `negotiation/negotiation-guide.md` to prepare counter
2. [ ] Research market rates (levels.fyi, Glassdoor)
3. [ ] Identify your walk-away and target numbers
4. [ ] Practice negotiation scripts
5. [ ] Get offer in writing before accepting
