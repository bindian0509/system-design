# STAR Stories Bank for Behavioral Interviews

> **Purpose**: A comprehensive bank of prepared stories for leadership/behavioral rounds.
> **Target**: 15-20 polished stories covering common themes.
> **Format**: STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) + Learning

---

## How to Use This Document

1. **Fill in each story template** with your actual experiences
2. **Practice each story** until you can tell it in 2-3 minutes
3. **Tag stories** with applicable themes (one story can cover multiple themes)
4. **Before each interview**, review which stories match the company's values

## Common Behavioral Questions by Company

### Google (Googleyness & Leadership)
1. Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority
2. Describe a situation where you had to deal with ambiguity
3. How do you handle disagreements with your team?
4. Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned

### Amazon (LP-focused)
1. Tell me about a time you took ownership of something outside your responsibility
2. Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information
3. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager
4. Give me an example of when you delivered results under pressure

### Microsoft (Growth Mindset)
1. Tell me about a time you received critical feedback
2. How do you approach learning new technologies?
3. Describe a situation where you had to adapt your approach
4. Tell me about a time you helped someone grow

### Generic EM Questions
1. How do you build trust with a new team?
2. Tell me about your management philosophy
3. How do you handle underperformers?
4. Describe your approach to 1:1s
5. How do you prioritize between competing demands?
6. Tell me about a difficult termination decision
7. How do you maintain technical depth as a manager?
8. Describe a time you had to deliver bad news

## Story Practice Checklist

- [ ] Can tell each story in under 3 minutes
- [ ] Each story has quantified results
- [ ] Using "I" not "we" throughout
- [ ] Have backup stories for each theme
- [ ] Practiced out loud at least 3 times per story
- [ ] Got feedback from a friend/mentor
- [ ] Can adapt each story based on follow-up questions
