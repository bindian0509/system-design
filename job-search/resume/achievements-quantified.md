## Impact Framework: The STAR-Q Method

For each achievement, capture:
- **S**ituation: Context and challenge
- **T**ask: Your specific responsibility
- **A**ction: What you did
- **R**esult: Outcome
- **Q**uantification: Numbers that prove impact
