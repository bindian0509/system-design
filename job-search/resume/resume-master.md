# Resume Optimization Tips

## For ATS Systems
1. Use standard section headers (Experience, Education, Skills)
2. Include keywords from job descriptions
3. Avoid tables, columns, graphics in PDF version
4. Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)

## For Recruiter Scanning (6-second test)
1. Name and title immediately visible
2. Current company prominently displayed
3. Key metrics in bold or highlighted
4. Clear progression visible

## Company-Specific Tailoring

### For Big Tech (Google, Amazon, Microsoft)
- Emphasize: Scale, distributed systems, technical depth
- Add: Leadership principles alignment (especially for Amazon)
- Tone: Technical credibility + collaboration

### For Fintech (PhonePe, Groww, CRED)
- Emphasize: Domain expertise, regulatory knowledge, scale
- Add: Specific payment metrics (TPS, failure rates, settlement times)
- Tone: Domain expert + business understanding

### For Startups (Zepto, Meesho)
- Emphasize: Speed, ownership, 0→1 building
- Add: Examples of ambiguity handling, rapid iteration
- Tone: Hands-on leader, builder mindset
