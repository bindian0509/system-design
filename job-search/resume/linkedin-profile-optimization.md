## Recommendations Strategy

### Who to Ask:
1. **Direct reports** - Speaks to your people management
2. **Engineering peers** - Speaks to collaboration
3. **Product/Design partners** - Speaks to cross-functional work
4. **Skip-level manager** - Speaks to impact and trust
5. **Engineers you've mentored** - Speaks to growth mindset

### Template Request Message:
```
Hi [Name],

I'm updating my LinkedIn profile as I explore new opportunities.
Would you be willing to write a brief recommendation focusing on
[specific aspect - e.g., "our collaboration on the settlement system"
or "my approach to team building"]?

Happy to reciprocate if helpful!
```

## Open to Work Settings

Navigate to: Profile → Open to Work → Get started

**Recommended Settings:**
- Job titles: Engineering Manager, Senior Engineering Manager, Staff Engineer, Principal Engineer
- Location: India (or specific cities + Remote)
- Start date: Immediately / Within 1 month
- Job types: Full-time
- Visibility: "Recruiters only" (if still at Razorpay during notice period)

---

## Profile Activity Strategy

### During Job Search:
1. **Post 1-2x per week** - Technical insights, leadership learnings
2. **Comment on 3-5 posts daily** - Stay visible in feeds
3. **Share industry news** - Shows you're plugged in
4. **Engage with target company content** - Gets you noticed

### Post Ideas:
- Lessons learned from scaling systems
- Team building insights
- Technical deep-dives (non-confidential)
- Book/article recommendations
- Industry commentary
