## 2. TDD (Technical Design Document Review)  Round :

We want to hire best candidates and as a part of our overall process for evaluating.
candidates for engineering manager and senior technology positions, we want to
ensure that we have an interview process that mirrors the work of an engineering as
closely as possible. We expect these positions to be able to review technical design.
documents for their team and guide the team towards good architectural choices
that produce good business outcomes.
A clear and well written document also creates powerful organizational leverage by
enabling effective asynchronous knowledge transfer.
In this round focus is on scalability design based on problem given. We encourage to
asks clarifying questions and make sure to keep conversation ongoing. Prior to
interview be sure to research on scalability concepts, caching, load balancing,
microservices and sharding etc. Knowledge of distributed systems, n-tier
architecture is very important. Justify your design wherever possible with data and
capacity planning. Use any tools of your choice to draw diagrams. You would get.
feedback and ensure you act on it. You need to drive the conversation with panellists
to closure.
